# Calypso Cycle → Sins (canon bridge)

Sins of a Capitalism Tycoon is a **simulation stage** set in the same commercial weather as
**The Calypso Cycle** (and the Galactic Confederation Review dossiers that share its law).

Source library (author workstation): `D:\repos\books\content\series\the-calypso-cycle`
— especially `references/` (registry, cargo, factions, stations, jump doctrine, arcs).

This is **not** a novel dump into the game repo. It is a **flavor and systems bible**:
names, doctrines, fee shapes, station tiers, and voices we may stage on the 100-star
Johnston seed without requiring the full plot of *Calypso* / *Personage* / *IBIC*.

## Provenance rule

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Lift **institutions** (CCA, Meridian, escrow, HILS, ST class) | Paste chapter prose wholesale |
| Adapt **station tiers** onto Astro roles | Relocate Duckville onto a Johnston star without a mapping note |
| Borrow **voices** (Ixa, Lydia, Torrik, Varr-adjacent) as `vox.*` | Claim Sins *is* James’s inheritance plot |
| Cite Review articles for public tone | Invent a second money stock called “credits” that secretly equals Ops+Core |

When fiction and sim disagree, **sim ledgers win**; flavor annotates.
